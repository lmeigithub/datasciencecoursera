This list describes the columns in the "tidy_data" data set:

subject: Integer from 1-30 indicating which the subject who performed the activities for each sample.

activity: A string indicating which of six activities (WALKING, WALKING_UPSTAIRS, WALKING_DOWNSTAIRS, SITTING, STANDING, or LAYING) that the subject was performing

tBodyAcc.mean...X: The average of the mean body acceleration signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAcc.mean...Y: The average of the mean body acceleration signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAcc.mean...Z: The average of the mean body acceleration signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAcc.std...X: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAcc.std...Y: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAcc.std...Z: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.mean...X: The average of the mean gravity acceleration signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.mean...Y: The average of the mean gravity acceleration signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.mean...Z: The average of the mean gravity acceleration signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.std...X: The average of the standard deviation of the gravity acceleration signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.std...Y: The average of the standard deviation of the gravity acceleration signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tGravityAcc.std...Z: The average of the standard deviation of the gravity acceleration signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.mean...X: The average of the mean body acceleration jerk signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.mean...Y: The average of the mean body acceleration jerk signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.mean...Z: The average of the mean body acceleration jerk signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.std...X: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration jerk signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.std...Y: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration jerk signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccJerk.std...Z: The average of the standard deviation of the body acceleration jerk signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyro.mean...X: The average of the mean body gyroscope signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyro.mean...Y: The average of the mean body gyroscope signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyro.mean...Z: The average of the mean body gyroscope signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyro.std...X: The average of the standard deviation of the body gyroscope signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyroJerk.mean...X: The average of the mean body gyroscope jerk signals in the X axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyroJerk.mean...Y: The average of the mean body gyroscope jerk signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyroJerk.std...Y: The average of the standard deviation of the body gyroscope jerk signals in the Y axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyGyroJerk.std...Z: The average of the standard deviation of the body gyroscope jerk signals in the Z axis recorded for the given subject and activity.

tBodyAccMag.mean: The average of the mean magnitude of the body acceleration signals.

tBodyAccMag.std: The standard deviation of the mean magnitude of the body acceleration signals.

tGravityAccMag.mean: The average of the mean magnitude of the gravity acceleration signals.
